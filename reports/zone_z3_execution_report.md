## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Audit

This report documents the rule-validation and governance audit of the third batch of six benchmark scenarios (S023–S028) representing the **Northern Dry Zone (Zone Z3)**. In strict adherence to project invariants:

1. **Zero Modifications to Frozen Engine:** The deterministic eligibility engine (`src/eligibility/engine.py`) and scheme master (`scheme_database/processed/scheme_master.json`) were executed byte-for-byte without altering any rule.
2. **Zero Synthetic Annotations:** No machine learning models were trained, no synthetic ratings were fabricated, and no expert scores were predicted.
3. **100% Assignment Table Fidelity:** Every scenario preserves its assigned district, crop, land size, land type, social category, tenure status, and primary policy challenge from `benchmark-design-v1.0.3`.
4. **Cross-Field Physical Area Consistency (S023):** Scenario S023 requested 1.41 ha micro-irrigation on a 3.5-acre parcel. The physical capacity check ($1.41 \text{ ha} \le 3.5 \times 0.404686 = 1.4164 \text{ ha}$) passes cleanly.
5. **Pure Tenant Governance Decoupling (Challenge 5, S026):** S026 evaluates a pure tenant without recorded title, triggering raw engine disqualification under PM-KISAN (`PMKISAN_PURE_TENANT_EXCLUSION`). For Central KCC-MISS, while the raw engine returned ELIGIBLE, the governance presentation places it in **Section B as `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`** because bank oral-lease tenant documentation or JLG membership is not on file.
6. **PMFBY Pomegranate Crop-Notification Policy Audit (S023 & S027):** For commercial pomegranate, the raw engine returned INELIGIBLE (`PMFBY_NON_NOTIFIED_CROP`) due to omission from the engine's static whitelist. The governance presentation classifies PMFBY as **Section B `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`** rather than a confirmed legal exclusion, preserving the raw result while logging seasonal notification verification.
7. **Community Pool Route & Beneficiary Threshold Discrepancy (Challenge 7, S028):** S028 tests the 10.0-acre boundary condition with 3 pooled OBC farmers (9.0 contiguous dryland acres). The raw engine returns `ELIGIBLE` (`GK_SCH010_DEVARAJ_URS_COMMUNITY_ROUTE_VALID`) using an encoded `>= 2` threshold, whereas benchmark design envisioned a minimum of 3. Because `SCH010_DEVARAJ_URS` is provisional, the governance safeguard places it in **Section B as `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`** with explicit missing verifications, shielding it from numerical scoring.
8. **Automated Parent Deduplication:** Abstract parents (`SCH008`, `SCH010`) are suppressed whenever their concrete child implementations are actionable.
