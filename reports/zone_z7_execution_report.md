This report documents the rule-validation and governance audit of the seventh batch of six benchmark scenarios (S047–S052) representing the **Southern Transition Zone (Zone Z7)**. In strict adherence to project invariants:

3. **100% Assignment Table Fidelity:** Every scenario preserves its assigned district, crop, land size, land type, social category, tenure status, and primary policy challenge from `benchmark-design-v1.0.4`.
