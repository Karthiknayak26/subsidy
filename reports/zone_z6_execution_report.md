## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Audit

This report documents the rule-validation and governance audit of the sixth batch of six benchmark scenarios (S041–S046) representing the **Southern Dry Zone (Zone Z6)**. In strict adherence to project invariants:

1. **Zero Modifications to Frozen Engine:** The deterministic eligibility engine (`src/eligibility/engine.py`) and scheme master (`scheme_database/processed/scheme_master.json`) were executed byte-for-byte without altering any rule.
2. **Zero Synthetic Annotations:** No machine learning models were trained, no synthetic ratings were fabricated, and no expert scores were predicted.
3. **100% Assignment Table Fidelity:** Every scenario preserves its assigned district, crop, land size, land type, social category, tenure status, and primary policy challenge from `benchmark-design-v1.0.3`.
4. **Explicit Availability Presentation Safeguard:** Wherever Section A (Confirmed Actionable Candidates) is displayed, it is made explicit that **statutory eligibility does not imply current application availability**. Operational availability is independently tracked via the discrete 4-state availability pipeline, preventing eligibility determinations from being mistaken for open application windows.
5. **PMKSY-PDMC Micro-Irrigation in Irrigated Canal Command (S041):** Medium holding (6.5 ac, Mandya); requested subsidized area of 2.50 ha strictly satisfies physical parcel capacity ($2.50 \le 6.5 \times 0.404686 = 2.6305$ ha) and statutory ceiling ($2.50 \le 5.00$ ha), qualifying under `PMKSY_LAND_RANGE_VALID` and `PMKSY_WATER_SOURCE_VERIFIED`.
6. **Pure Tenant Gating & Tenancy Documentation (Challenge 5, S042):** Pure tenant cultivating 3.5 acres wetland paddy in Mysuru without registered title. PM-KISAN strictly disqualifies pure tenants (`PMKISAN_PURE_TENANT_EXCLUSION`). PMFBY and Central KCC evaluate raw `ELIGIBLE`, but governance decouples both to **Section B as `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`** without numerical scoring pending tenant sowing certificates from the Village Accountant and official Kharif 2026 Gram Panchayat Insurance Unit notification orders.
7. **Valmiki ST Ganga Kalyana Individual Route Safeguard (S043):** Marginal ST dryland farmer (1.8 ac, Chamarajanagar) evaluates raw `ELIGIBLE` under `SCH010_VALMIKI` (`GK_SCH010_VALMIKI_INDIVIDUAL_ROUTE_VALID`). Because `SCH010_VALMIKI` is provisional in registry and requires source-adjacency field verification, governance strictly routes it to **Section B as `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`** without numerical scoring. Abstract parent `SCH010` is suppressed from recommendations.
8. **Relief vs. Reimbursable Debt on Pure Tenancy (Challenge 2 & Challenge 5, S044):** Pure tenant wetland paddy cultivator (2.0 ac, Mandya) in acute distress requests non-repayable income support. PM-KISAN strictly disqualifies pure tenants (`PMKISAN_PURE_TENANT_EXCLUSION`), demonstrating statutory exclusion overriding stated immediate need. In accordance with Annotation Protocol v1.1 Rule 2, reimbursable credit (KCC-MISS) must NOT receive clinical score 3; furthermore, tenant status routes KCC to Section B pending oral lease / JLG documentation.
9. **Krishi Bhagya Rainwater Harvesting in Southern Dry Zone & Ambedkar Safeguard (S045):** Marginal SC dryland farmer (1.5 ac, Mysuru) cleanly qualifies for Krishi Bhagya farm pond package (`KRISHI_BHAGYA_DRYLAND_VERIFIED`, `KRISHI_BHAGYA_DISTRICT_NOTIFIED`, `KRISHI_BHAGYA_ACREAGE_VALID`) in Section A, with the historical 90% SC subsidy rate qualified as pending FY 2026-27 GO verification. Ambedkar Ganga Kalyana raw engine returns `ELIGIBLE`, but in strict adherence to the S029 governance precedent, safeguard decouples it to **Section B as `REQUIRES_VERIFICATION`** without numerical scoring due to dual unverified conditions: absence of recorded perennial water source adjacency and unverified Mysuru rural annual family income ceiling rules (recorded income of ₹1.10 L evaluated against unverified rural ₹1.50 L vs state ₹2.00 L ceiling orders). Abstract parent `SCH010` is suppressed.
10. **Universal Diagnostic Service Access (S046):** Medium sugarcane grower (7.0 ac canal command, Kollegal, Chamarajanagar) qualifies for 100% free Soil Health Card diagnostic profiling (`SHC_UNIVERSAL_ACCESS_VERIFIED`), directly meeting primary need in Section A. Canal irrigated status strictly disqualifies Krishi Bhagya and Ganga Kalyana.
11. **Reconciled Denominators & Parent Suppression:** Every scenario evaluates exactly 16 raw scheme records. Scenarios S041, S042, S044, and S046 display **15 visible schemes** (`SCH008` suppressed; `SCH010` retained as ineligible). Scenarios S043 and S045 display **14 visible schemes** (`SCH008` and `SCH010` both suppressed because corporation children evaluate as raw `ELIGIBLE`).
