# Project Scope

## In Scope
- **Domain Focus:** Agricultural schemes, subsidies, inputs, equipment, and financial assistance relevant to farmers (with an initial focus on Karnataka and Central Government programs).
- **Dual-Stage System Architecture:**
  1. *Deterministic Rule/Eligibility Filtering:* Strictly based on verified government guidelines.
  2. *Machine Learning Recommendation / Ranking:* Ranking filtered or candidate schemes based on farmer attributes, operational requirements, and relevance.
- **Explainability:** Providing transparent rationale for why a scheme is recommended, the exact subsidy/benefit formula, required paperwork, and the official application portal.
- **Future Modalities:** Multilingual support (Kannada primary) and low-connectivity access channels (IVR/SMS) for basic/feature phones.

## Out of Scope (Current Phase)
- **Automated Scheme Approval:** The system does not approve applications or disburse funds; it acts purely as a discovery, guidance, and recommendation platform.
- **Invention of Eligibility Rules by ML:** Machine learning models will NOT predict or invent eligibility thresholds or monetary subsidy figures. All legal rules are sourced strictly from government gazettes/portals.
- **Synthetic Scheme Data Creation:** No fictitious schemes or fake policy records will be introduced.
