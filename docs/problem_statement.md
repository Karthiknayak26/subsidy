# Problem Statement

## Context
Agriculture is the livelihood foundation for millions of Indian farmers. Central and State governments offer a diverse spectrum of subsidies, schemes, financial grants, and developmental support programs (e.g., input subsidies, drip irrigation grants, machinery custom hiring, interest subventions, crop insurance).

## The Core Challenge
Despite the availability of these programs, there exists a severe information disconnect between the governing bodies offering benefits and the farming communities eligible to receive them.

Key friction points:
1. **Awareness Deficit:** Farmers focus heavily on daily field management and seasonal operations, leaving little time or bandwidth to track government notifications and portal updates.
2. **Information Fragmentation:** Scheme details are scattered across disparate departments (Agriculture, Horticulture, Sericulture, Animal Husbandry, Water Resources) at both Central and State levels.
3. **Complex Eligibility Criteria:** Schemes involve composite constraints (land size thresholds, farmer categories such as Small/Marginal/SC/ST/General, water sources, crop specificities) that are difficult for farmers to parse without assistance.
4. **Economic Burden:** When farmers fail to discover applicable subsidies in time, they frequently purchase agricultural inputs or equipment at full out-of-pocket cost or incur high-interest non-institutional debt.

## Objective
To build an intelligent, transparent, and personalized recommendation pipeline that ingests verified farmer profiles, evaluates factual eligibility constraints against verified government schemes, and ranks the most relevant assistance programs for the farmer.
